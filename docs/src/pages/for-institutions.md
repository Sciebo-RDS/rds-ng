We know managing research data across departments, faculties, and entire institutions is a complex challenge. _bridgit_ provides a flexible, ready-to-integrate, open-access solution for research data management in cloud infrastructures.

_bridgit_ is built to strengthen the entire research ecosystem Whether you manage a university cloud, run a library service, or handle sensitive data outside academia, _bridgit_ gives your institution a flexible foundation for transparent, FAIR, and secure research data management.

## For Universities and Academic Institutions

_bridgit_ integrates directly into your existing university cloud (such as sciebo) and turns it into a full-featured research data connection hub to external data repositories.

**Your advantages**:

- Offer an in-house, GDPR-compliant RDM solution for all disciplines
- Reduce administrative overhead by connecting existing tools and repositories
- Enable seamless data sharing, archiving, and publication from one interface
- Strengthen your institution’s commitment to Open Science and FAIR principles
  _bridgit_ makes research data management a built-in service of your university and doesn’t add an extra burden for researchers within their research data workflow.

## For Service Entities (e.g. Libraries)

Services, such as libraries, play a key role in guiding researchers through the data lifecycle. _bridgit_ makes that process easier, smarter, and more consistent across departments.

**Your advantages**:

- Centralize data management plans and project documentation
- Simplify metadata curation and annotation with flexible templates
- Manage and archive large annotated datasets
- Monitor compliance with institutional and funding requirements
  With _bridgit_, support teams can focus on building capacity without chasing file versions.

## For Non-Academic Institutions

Whether you handle biological samples, satellite data, or industrial research results, _bridgit_ adapts to your workflows while ensuring security and traceability.

**Your advantages**:

- Configurate and manage (sensitive) datasets securely within your own infrastructure
- Collaborate with academic and public partners on shared projects
- Maintain full control over access, storage, and data lineage
- Integrate your existing tools via open connectors and APIs
  _bridgit_ literally bridges the gap between research, innovation, and industry collaboration, tailored to your needs: safely and efficiently.
