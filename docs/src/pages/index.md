# Meet _bridgit_ ! Your new data juggling assistant to share important research data.

<div>
<span style={{textAlign: 'center', width: '20%', float: 'right', margin: '2%' }}>
  <img class="bg-white"  src="/img/juggler.svg" />
</span>

**Let us introduce you two.**

Think of _bridgit_ as your new, multitasking research partner. The app will support you from the very first draft of your dataset to publishing it with the scientific community - _bridgit_ accompanies you step by step without compromising any of your effort. And the best part? _bridgit_ is fully integrated into your secure university cloud environment, making sharing easy and safe.

_bridgit_ emerged from a DFG-funded project and is administrated by the University of Münster, with the University of Potsdam as its scientific consultant. The collaboration has been so successful that _bridgit_ was piloted in the sciebo Hochschulcloud at the University of Münster, where it proved its value in real-world academic settings and quickly began attracting the interest of other institutions.

## Why share your research data at all?
### We are working together - for the bigger picture.
<span style={{textAlign: 'center', width: '30%', float: 'right', margin: '2%' }}>
<img class="bg-white"  src="/img/fair.png" />
</span>
Open science drives transparency, collaboration, and innovation in research. By making data accessible, interoperable, and reusable, the FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) principles ensure that research outputs can be discovered, shared, and built upon by the entire research community¹.

## How does _bridgit_ work exactly?

### One word: easy. Another one: integrated into everyone’s workflow.

_bridgit_ supports you from the very first step of preparing and saving your data through to securely sharing it with the scientific community. All without compromising your control or exposing your data to the risk of misuse. Integrated directly into your secure university cloud, _bridgit_ turns data management into a seamless part of your research routine.

<div style={{textAlign: 'center', width: '70%', float: 'none', margin: 'auto' }}>
  <img class="bg-white"  src="/img/rds-core.png" />
</div>

_bridgit_ enables you to:

- prepare and annotate datasets
- write and maintain data management plans
- upload projects, data objects, and metadata annotations
- connect directly with external research data services such as Zenodo or OSF to upload your data directly into the repositories

## Who benefits from using _bridgit_?

#### First and formost: The entire science community!

<div style={{textAlign: 'center', width: '70%', float: 'none', margin: 'auto' }}>
  <img class="bg-white"  src="/img/bridgit-process.jpg" />
</div>

But specifically, _bridgit_ is mainly designed for three main groups: researchers across all disciplines at universities and institutes who need secure data management, academic institutions who want to extend their (e.g.) Nextcloud environment with a research-focused solution and non-academic companies who are interested in integrating or extending _bridgit_ to meet specific organizational needs (or connect their work to the scientific community).

## Beta testing

We invite you to join the _bridgit_ beta test! As early adopters, you'll have the chance to use the app before its official release. Your feedback is invaluable. Whether you're working on a dataset now or will do so in the future, we encourage you to test the app and provide your thoughts.
